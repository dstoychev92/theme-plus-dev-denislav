# Layout and components

- Width: `layout_site_width`, with the large margin (`grid_spacing_margin`) and medium gutter.
- Section rhythm: `section_rhythm_default`.
- Radii: `rounding_sm`, `rounding_md`, `rounding_lg`. Cards take the small radius (`card_rounding`),
  banners the medium (`banner_default_rounding`).
- Buttons, form fields, and tabs are pills (`button_rounding`, `form_field_rounding`, `tab_rounding`).
- Swatches are circles. Badges take the small radius.
- Cards: portrait (`card_aspect_ratio`).
- Accordions use plus and minus. Tabs use an underline. Carousels use line pagination and bare controls.
- Decoration: the asterisk mark (`decoration_shape`), in the muted color.
- Icons: the modern set. The cart icon is a bag, and add to cart opens the drawer.

Rule: round controls, square-ish cards. A new component follows the same split.
