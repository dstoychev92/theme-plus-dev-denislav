# Signature

Draft: read from the live homepage and product pages.

## Before / with BOOM!

The one move: a problem and its fix set as a pair. A small accent-font label "ПРЕДИ" over the
problem, then "С boom!" over the change. Live in `section-tabs` on the homepage ("Енергиен срив" →
"По-стабилна енергия и ритъм през деня") and in "BOOM! VS ОБИКНОВЕНО КАФЕ" on the bundle page.

- **Why it is the signature:** it is the brand's problem → solution voice made visible. It names
  the reader's moment first
- **Where:** homepage once, product page once. Never in the cart or the footer
- **Rules:** the "С boom!" side sits on the stronger surface (`scheme-opposite` or primary). One
  problem per pair. The fix states a change the facts back up, never a cure
- **First time right:** build it with Analog's own tabs or comparison blocks, labels in the accent
  type preset, before a custom section
