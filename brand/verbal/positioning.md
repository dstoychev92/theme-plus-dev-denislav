# Positioning

Draft: extracted from the live copy. The client confirms it on the review page.

## What and for whom

BOOM! makes coffee that does more than wake you up: protein for the body, adaptogens for the mind.
It speaks to busy, active adults in Bulgaria, Greece and Romania who skip breakfast, train, or work
long mental hours.

## What it stands for

- **Two formulas, one mission.** "Не смесваме протеин и адаптогени в един продукт — всеки прави
  едно нещо перфектно." A neighbor selling a single "functional coffee" cannot say this. Evidence:
  the two product lines and `page.science`.
- **We prove, we do not claim.** "Не твърдим. Доказваме." Every benefit sits next to its EFSA
  claim or its study. Evidence: `product.brain-coffee`, `page.science` FAQ.

## The reader's moment

Morning with no time for breakfast, before or after training, or the afternoon slump at a desk.
They want steady energy, no crash, no jitters, and fewer pills.
