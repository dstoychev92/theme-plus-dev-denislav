# Voice

Draft: extracted from the live Bulgarian copy. Not confirmed by the client yet.

## Voice (fixed)

| Trait | Means | Does not mean | From the store |
|---|---|---|---|
| Punchy | Short lines, full stops for rhythm, the BOOM! burst | Shouting every line | "Разбъркай. Отпий. Започни." |
| Friendly, on first-name terms | Speaks to "ти" in selling copy | Slang or emoji | "Остави имейла си и получи 7% отстъпка веднага." |
| Evidence-led | A benefit comes with its ingredient, dose, or study | Medical promises | "Не твърдим. Доказваме." |
| Problem → solution | Names the everyday problem, then what changes | Fear or shame | "ПРЕДИ: Енергиен срив / С boom!: По-стабилна енергия" |

## Tone by moment

| Moment | Tone |
|---|---|
| Selling (homepage, product) | Bold caps headline, one-line benefit, a number |
| Explaining (science, FAQ) | Calm and precise. Doses in g and mg. Cite the source |
| Helping (shipping, returns) | Plain facts, prices, days. Use "Вие" as the FAQ does today |
| Health caution | Neutral. Advise a doctor. Never minimise |
| Thank you, offers | Warm and short. "Без спам, обещаваме." |

## Mechanics

- Headlines and buttons in capitals (set by `type_uppercase_*` settings, not typed in caps where the setting does it)
- Body in sentence case
- The brand is "BOOM!" in headlines and "Boom" in running text, such as "Boom Protein Coffee"
- Numbers as digits with the unit and no space for grams: "10g", "150mg". Currency after the amount: "50€", "375 lei"
- Rhythm: three short sentences of two to three words each ("Разбъркай. Отпий. Започни.")
- No emoji
- Uneven today: "ти" in selling copy, "Вие" in the FAQ. Draft: keep both as they are
